About Me

Hello! My name is Sathish, and I am from Tamilnadu, India, currently based in Dublin, Ireland. I completed my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science at Thiagarajar College and my Master's degree in Computing (Cloud Technologies) at Atlantic Technological University.

Professionally, I am a DevOps and Cloud Engineer with a passion for building reliable, scalable, and automated cloud-native platforms. I have hands-on experience with AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, Jenkins, Argo CD, and GitHub Actions, along with monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, and Datadog. Outside of my professional and academic pursuits, I enjoy exploring cloud technologies, automation, and emerging DevOps practices. I am particularly interested in Kubernetes, GitOps, Infrastructure as Code, cloud security, and observability, and I continuously work on hands-on projects to strengthen my technical skills. I also enjoy travelling, staying active, and learning new technologies, which help me maintain a balance between professional growth and personal development.
